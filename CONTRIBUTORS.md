# Contributors

MIAOW is maintained by the Numerical Simulation Laboratory at the University of Saskatchewan, with [Tristan Montoya](https://tjbmontoya.com) as its current principal developer. The following people, listed alphabetically by surname, have contributed to MIAOW through code, documentation, testing, scientific guidance, project management, and other activities:

- Mandana Bidarvand, University of Saskatchewan
- Tristan Montoya, University of Saskatchewan
- Raymond Spiteri, University of Saskatchewan

These individuals are collectively referred to as the *MIAOW contributors*. If you have contributed to MIAOW and would like to be added to this list, please [open an issue](https://github.com/uofs-simlab/miaow-framework/issues) or submit a pull request to update this file.
